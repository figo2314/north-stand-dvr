# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Chinese football fans, especially Arsenal supporters, who cannot watch European kickoffs live because most matches begin late at night or early in the morning. They use the product on a personal Windows computer, leave it running overnight, and return to it at breakfast with limited time and a strong desire to avoid seeing the result before playback.

## Product Purpose

North Stand DVR records scheduled matches locally, protects every browsing surface from score leaks, and makes last night's match available as a single, calm click the next morning. Success means the user can schedule a match once, trust that it will be recorded, and start watching without encountering a score in a title, thumbnail, notification, browser tab, or accidental glance.

## Brand Personality

Composed, protective, quietly obsessive. The voice is concise and matchday-aware without pretending to be an official club product. It should feel like a trusted piece of personal kit rather than a sports news site.

## Anti-references

No live-score dashboards, betting-style tickers, flashing alerts, news cards, autoplay highlights, or official club branding. Do not imitate a television EPG interface. Avoid decorative football clichés and interfaces that make the result the primary content.

## Design Principles

1. Protect first: every result-bearing surface is hidden until the user explicitly reveals it.
2. One clear morning action: the primary experience is opening a recording and pressing play.
3. Trust through status: recording health, free space, next task, and failures must be legible at a glance.
4. Configuration, not code: stream coordinates, mask area, storage path, and schedule are user-editable.
5. Honest limits: if FFmpeg, storage, or a stream source is unavailable, say so before the match starts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard operable, visible focus states, semantic labels, reduced-motion support, and text contrast that remains readable in both the bright morning and late-night recording contexts. Color must never be the only way to communicate recording or spoiler state.
